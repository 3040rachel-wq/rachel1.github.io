🪜 Crossclimb

Climb a word ladder, one letter change at a time.

How to play

Answer the 5 clues to fill the middle of the ladder. Then reorder the 5 words (using the arrows) so each one differs from its neighbor by exactly one letter. Finally, solve the combined clue to unlock the two locked words at the top and bottom.
