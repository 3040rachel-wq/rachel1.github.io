▦ Patches

Carve the grid into rectangles, one clue per patch.

How to play

Press on an empty cell and drag to another to draw a rectangular patch, then release to place it. Every patch must contain exactly one numbered clue, and its area (number of cells) must equal that number. Some clues also show a small shape badge — ▭ for wide, a rotated ▭ for tall, and ▢ for square — meaning the patch's proportions must match too; clues with no badge accept any proportions. Tap a placed patch to remove it. You win once the whole grid is divided into patches that each satisfy their clue.
