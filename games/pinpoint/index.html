🎯 Pinpoint

Five clues, one hidden category. Guess it in as few clues as you can.

How to play

A category is hidden behind five clues, revealed one at a time from most obscure to most obvious. Type your guess for the category and submit. Guess right after clue 1 for 5 points, clue 2 for 4 points, and so on down to 1 point after all five clues. No time limit — take your time.

Worth: 5 points
