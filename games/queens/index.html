G Games / Queens ✕

👑 Queens

One crown per row, column & color region — none touching.

How to play

Place exactly one crown (👑) in every row, every column, and every colored region. Two crowns may never touch, not even diagonally. Tap a cell to cycle it empty → ✕ → 👑 → empty; the ✕ is just a scratch mark to note cells you've ruled out and has no effect on the rules. Use Auto-check to catch conflicts as you go.
