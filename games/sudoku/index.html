⚏ Mini Sudoku

Fill the 6×6 grid so every row, column and box has 1–6, once each.

How to play

Select a cell, then tap a number. Every row, column, and 2×3 box must contain the digits 1 through 6 exactly once. Given numbers are locked. Use Auto-check to catch mistakes as you go.
