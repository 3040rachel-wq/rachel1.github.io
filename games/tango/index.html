☯ Tango

Balance the suns and moons. No three in a row.

How to play

Tap a cell to cycle it empty → Sun → Moon → empty. Fill the 6×6 grid so that:

• Each row and column has exactly 3 suns and 3 moons.

• No 3 identical symbols are adjacent in a row or column, horizontally or vertically.

• No two rows are identical, and no two columns are identical.

• Cells joined by = must match; cells joined by × must differ. These markers are fixed and always shown.

Given cells are locked. Use Auto-check to catch mistakes as you go.
