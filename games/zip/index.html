⚡ Zip

Draw one continuous path through every cell, hitting the numbered dots in order.

How to play

Press and drag from dot 1 through orthogonally adjacent cells (up, down, left, right — no diagonals) to draw a single path that fills the whole grid. You must reach dots 2, 3, 4… in ascending order along the way; the last dot must be the final cell. Bold lines between cells are walls — the path can never cross them, so route around instead. Drag back onto your previous cell to undo a step. Release to pause; press the end of your path again to keep going.
